About ACV

Communities most affected by crisis should lead their own recovery.

Africa Community Village (ACV) is a youth-led nonprofit founded in the Democratic Republic of Congo, working at the intersection of education, environment, agriculture, health and livelihoods across Eastern DRC.

Our history

A response born from the place it serves

ACV was founded by a group of young Congolese leaders who saw, first-hand, that the communities most affected by conflict, poverty and environmental degradation were rarely the ones designing the response to it.

We chose to root our work in Eastern DRC — one of the world's most complex humanitarian contexts — precisely because that is where meaningful, community-owned change is hardest to build and most needed. Rather than a single intervention, ACV promotes community-driven, climate-resilient and inclusive development through five integrated program pillars that meet people where the need is most acute: in the classroom, on the land, in the clinic, and in the local economy.

Today, that founding instinct still shapes every program we run: listen first, build local capacity always, and measure success by what continues after we step back.

Where we are headed
Our vision

Resilient, healthy, empowered communities — led by their own youth

We work toward a future where communities across Africa are resilient, healthy and empowered: where youth lead sustainable development, environmental protection and inclusive socio-economic transformation, rather than waiting for it to arrive from elsewhere.

Our mission

To empower youth and vulnerable communities through education, sustainable agriculture, environmental protection, health promotion and socio-economic development initiatives that strengthen resilience, improve livelihoods, and promote peace and sustainability.

“Resilient, healthy, and empowered communities in Africa, where youth lead sustainable development.”
ACV Vision Statement
What guides us

Eight values, one working culture

These are not framed posters on a wall — they are the criteria our teams use to design a program, choose a partner, or settle a disagreement in the field.

01

Community Leadership

Communities are the authors of their own development, not the audience for someone else's plan.

02

Youth Empowerment

Young people are treated as capable leaders today, not as beneficiaries to be developed for tomorrow.

03

Equity & Inclusion

Programs are designed so that women, youth and marginalised groups have a genuine seat at the table.

04

Sustainability

Solutions are built to outlast the funding cycle, rooted in local capacity and local materials.

05

Integrity & Accountability

We report honestly on what works, what doesn't, and where resources go.

06

Collaboration

Government, civil society, traditional leaders and international partners all have a role to play.

07

Resilience & Adaptability

Programs are designed to keep functioning when shocks — conflict, climate, displacement — hit.

08

Respect for Indigenous Knowledge

Generations of local ecological and agricultural knowledge are treated as expertise, not folklore.
