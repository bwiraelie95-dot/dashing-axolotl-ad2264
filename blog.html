Miti Alliance: pupils plant the next generation of forest

At partner schools around Goma, the Miti Alliance programme turns a corner of the schoolyard into a working tree nursery — and turns pupils into the people who tend it.

The model is deliberately simple: pupils raise seedlings from seed, track their growth as part of a basic ecology curriculum, and eventually transplant them to degraded plots nominated by the community itself. Teachers report that the most enthusiastic participants are rarely the strongest students academically — tending a living seedling rewards a different kind of attention.

For ACV, the programme does double duty: it is an environmental-restoration activity and a youth-leadership one at the same time, which is exactly the kind of overlap our five-pillar model is designed to produce.
