Support the work

Ways to give

ACV is a young organisation and we keep our giving process direct and personal — write to us and we will share the option that suits you, with full transparency on where funds go.

01

Direct bank or mobile-money transfer

Contact us for current account and mobile-money details for one-off or recurring contributions.

02

Program-specific giving

Earmark a contribution to a specific pillar — a tree nursery, a savings cooperative, a health volunteer kit.

03

In-kind & institutional support

Equipment, technical expertise or multi-year institutional funding — speak to our partnerships team directly.

Institutional & technical partners

Become a partner

We work with NGOs, foundations, research institutions and diaspora networks on co-designed programs, technical partnerships and joint funding proposals.

If you represent an organisation, please use the form above with “Program partnership” or “Funding & institutional support” as the reason for contact, and include a short description of your organisation so we can route it to the right team member.

Due-diligence pack available

Governance documents, recent accounts and our M&E framework are available to institutional partners on request.

Co-design, not sub-contracting

We partner best on programs we help shape from the start — see our Approach page for how that works in practice.

Africa Community Village

A youth-led nonprofit working at the intersection of education, environment, food security, health and livelihoods in Eastern DR Congo — built on the belief that communities most affected by crisis should lead their own recovery.
