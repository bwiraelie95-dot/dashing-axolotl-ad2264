Youth Leadership Academy — a weekend cohort of young facilitators in training.
Miti Alliance School Programme — pupils planting tree seedlings on their own school grounds.
A regenerative-agriculture demonstration plot, mid-season, shared between three farmer households.
Community health volunteers preparing household visit routes for the week.
A savings & livelihoods cooperative meeting in Himbi, Goma.
A community patrol mapping forest-edge encroachment near Virunga National Park.
